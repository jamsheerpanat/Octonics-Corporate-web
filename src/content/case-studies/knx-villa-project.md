## Project Overview

This luxury villa in Messila, Kuwait, was designed to showcase the power of a *true KNX-based automation system*.  
Octonics delivered a **complete smart home ecosystem** that combines reliability, comfort, and style.

> “Install once — forget forever.”  
> Every component is KNX-certified, ensuring long-term stability and expansion capability.

---

## System Architecture

The automation backbone was built using **KNX** for distributed control and **DALI** for lighting precision.  
Integration highlights include:

- **Lighting Control (DALI2):** Individual and group dimming with adaptive daylight response.  
- **HVAC Management:** Temperature zones linked with presence sensors and schedules.  
- **Curtain & Blind Automation:** Timed, manual, and light-based controls.  
- **RTI Visualization:** Centralized dashboard for control via iPad and wall-mounted panels.  
- **Voice Integration:** Amazon Alexa for voice-based scene control.  

---

## Panel & Programming Details

Octonics designed and programmed two fully assembled panels, including:
- Theben PS640 power supplies
- ABB KNX actuators and line couplers
- Jung keypads and thermostats
- RTI XP-6s controller for logic and integration

Wiring was tested and labeled for future upgrades, and **ETS programming** was fully documented.
