---

## 🌍 What Is an ERP System?

**Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)** is an integrated software solution that manages core business functions like accounting, procurement, HR, inventory, projects, and sales — all from one centralized system.  

A well-implemented ERP helps organizations:
- Eliminate manual data entry and duplication  
- Improve collaboration between departments  
- Gain real-time insights into operations  
- Standardize workflows for better control and decision-making  

However, for Kuwaiti enterprises, **a generic ERP often isn’t enough.**
