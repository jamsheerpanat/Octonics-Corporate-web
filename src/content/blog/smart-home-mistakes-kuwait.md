# Top 10 Smart Home Mistakes People Make in Kuwait

Here are the most common mistakes:

1. Choosing Wi-Fi instead of KNX  
2. No lighting circuit planning  
3. Wrong AC thermostat wiring  
4. No automation panel space  
5. Not using DALI lighting  
6. No documentation  
7. Hiring non-certified installers  
8. Overloading power supplies  
9. No networking structure  
10. No UPS backup  

Avoid these for a **stable long-term system**.
