# Zigbee, Z-Wave or KNX — What’s Best for Kuwait Homes?

Here’s a Kuwait-specific comparison:

## KNX (Best for Villas)
- Wired  
- Most stable  
- Ideal for lighting & AC  
- Works offline  

## Zigbee (Great for Small Retrofits)
- Wireless  
- App-friendly  
- Affordable  

## Z-Wave (Good for Add-Ons)
- Strong range  
- Good for sensors, locks, accessories  

## Best Solution
Use **KNX as the backbone**, wireless systems as **supporting add-ons**.
